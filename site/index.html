The calendar

A clock, not a grid.

Six hours at a time, laid out like a real clock face. Arcs are sized by duration and labelled along the curve; overlaps share a ring side by side; your phone use per hour runs around the edge.

  • Drag the rings to move through the day, across midnight
  • Tap anything to open it or tick it off
  • Add a quick task, or one with a time and a reminder

College

The semester, handled.

Your weekly timetable with one-off changes, exams with countdowns and a readiness score, assignments, marks, a study timer, flashcards and a revision planner that finds free time before each exam.

  • Move, cancel or add a class for a single day
  • Readiness per exam from topics, study time and flashcards
  • Reminders the evening before and an hour before

Attendance import

Screenshots in, numbers out.

Most college portals don't export anything. So BOVI reads a small file that any AI chat app can make from your screenshots.

  1. Screenshot each subject's attendance page on your college portal.
  2. Open ChatGPT (or Claude, or Gemini), attach the screenshots and paste the prompt.
  3. Copy the JSON it replies with, or save it as attendance.json.
  4. In BOVI: Attendance → Update from portal → paste or open the file, check the preview, done.

The app has the same steps and a button that sends the prompt straight to your AI app. The file is read only on your phone.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. BOVI is free and open source under the MIT licence.

Why does Android warn me when installing?

BOVI is downloaded from GitHub rather than the Play Store, so Android asks you to allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app. That's normal for apps installed this way.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. BOVI is Android only for now.

How do I update?

Download the newest APK and open it. It installs over the old version and keeps your data.

My college portal looks different. Will the import work?

Usually, yes: the AI reads whatever the screenshots show and fills in the same format. Always check the preview against your portal before saving.
